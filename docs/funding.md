# Where the money and the traction might come from

**Status: research, checked at source 2026-09-08.** Every date and figure below
was read on the funder's own page, not recalled. Where something could not be
confirmed it says so. Nothing here has been applied for.

---

## The two facts that decide everything else

**1. You are an individual, not a company.** Most health-innovation money in the
UK is written for registered businesses — SBRI contracts, Innovate UK grants,
accelerator places. This is not a detail to work around later; it removes about
two thirds of the obvious list.

**2. You are in Wales.** Almost everything with "NHS" in the name that gets
recommended online is England-only: the NHS Innovation Service, the Health
Innovation Networks, the NHS Innovation Accelerator. Wales has its own,
smaller, and in some ways more approachable set.

Together these mean **NLnet is the only route on this page you can walk straight
into today, as yourself, with no company and no partner.** Everything else needs
either an organisation to front it or a conversation first.

## 1. NLnet — Restack. Deadline **3 November 2026, noon CET**

**Verified on their own pages today.** Two corrections to what this project has
been assuming:

**The fund has changed name and shape.** NGI Zero Commons Fund ran its
*thirteenth and final call*, closing 1 June 2026. The successor is **Restack**,
whose first call opened **3 September 2026** — five days ago — with a deadline
of **3 November 2026 12:00 CET**. So the date in your head is right; the fund
behind it is not the one you were reading about.

- **€5,000 to €50,000** for a first proposal.
- **€7 million total, awarded between now and 2030**, so there will be further
  calls if this one is missed.
- **Individuals may apply.** Their eligibility page: *"There are no categorical
  exclusions of persons who may not receive support."* They accept individuals,
  informal groups and legal entities alike.
- **Everything must be open source**, which this already is.
- The UK is associated to Horizon Europe, so the *"inhabitants of the EU and
  countries associated to Horizon Europe are given priority"* clause works in
  your favour rather than against you.

**The honest risk is scope.** Restack's stated goal is *"to help deliver, mature
and scale operational internet commons across the technology stack"*, and it
names *"innovations and enhancements to core internet infrastructure,
alternatives to closed online technologies"*. That is more infrastructure-shaped
than the old Commons Fund was. Hearth is an application sitting on somebody
else's infrastructure, and the application will have to argue that what it is
really building is a **pattern** — person-held records with no operator — rather
than one care app.

That argument is available and it is not a stretch. But it is the argument, and
it should be written deliberately rather than assumed.

> ⚠️ **Read the Restack call text properly before writing anything.** The scope
> paragraph above came from their summary page. The call itself will be more
> specific, and the difference between "in scope" and "nearly in scope" is the
> whole application.

## 2. Wales

### Bevan Commission — Bevan Exemplars

A 12-month programme with mentoring, run out of Swansea University, for taking a
health or care idea into practice. Culturally it is the best fit on this page:
it is about prudent healthcare, it is Welsh, and it is aimed at ideas rather
than products.

**But you are not eligible on your own.** It is open to people *"working in NHS
Wales, Social Care Wales, Local Government or within Regional Partnership
Boards"*, and projects *"must be supported by an NHS Wales Health Board or Trust
and/or your organisation."*

So this is a route that needs **one NHS Wales or social care person willing to
put their name to it.** That is a smaller ask than it sounds — an Exemplar is a
professional developing an idea, and Hearth could be that person's idea to
develop with you. It also solves a problem money cannot: it puts somebody inside
the system next to the project.

Cohort dates move each year; the last one visible closed in July. **Worth an
email to `bevan-commission@swansea.ac.uk` asking when the next cohort opens and
whether a non-NHS technical partner can be part of an application.**

[Bevan Exemplars](https://bevancommission.org/programmes/bevan-exemplars/) ·
[Cohort 9 call](https://bevancommission.org/apply-to-join-bevan-exemplars-cohort-9/)

### Life Sciences Hub Wales

The connector organisation for health and care innovation in Wales. They run a
**funding support service** that helps innovators find and win grants, across
*"health, social care, academia and business"* — note that it says innovators,
not companies.

Their main money route is **SBRI**, run from the SBRI Centre of Excellence
hosted by Betsi Cadwaladr and funded by Welsh Government. SBRI is a *contract*
for R&D against a stated challenge, not a grant for your own idea, and it
generally needs a business. Useful later, not now.

**The realistic first move here is the conversation, not the application.**

[Life Sciences Hub Wales](https://lshubwales.com/) ·
[SBRI](https://lshubwales.com/small-business-research-initiative-sbri)

### Digital Health and Care Wales

The national body for digital health in Wales. Its strategy talks about
*"co-development and innovation with early engagement on emerging
technologies"*, which is the right language, and it is a far smaller and more
reachable organisation than anything in England.

Note: the **Digital Health Ecosystem Wales programme is closed**, so any
recommendation pointing you there is out of date.

[DHCW](https://dhcw.nhs.wales/)

### UNITE European Digital Health Valleys — live, and Welsh

Welsh Government has *"funding of more than £700,000 to support participation"*
in a Horizon Europe project called UNITE, and is explicitly inviting *"Welsh
SMEs, health boards, universities and innovation bodies"*. Scope is ageing
populations and workforce pressure.

Deadlines are not published on the announcement. There was a free webinar on
3 September 2026, and **Business Wales on 03000 6 03000** is the stated contact.
**Ring them.** This is the newest live Welsh thing found and it costs a phone
call to find out whether an individual counts.

[Welsh Government announcement](https://www.gov.wales/digital-health-eu-funding-scheme-open-welsh-applications)

## 3. England, and why most of it is shut

The **NHS Innovation Service** is free, is *"your gateway to innovation in the
NHS"*, and supports *"all types of healthcare innovators, from start-up to
established enterprise"*. It is worth registering on — it is a front door, not a
fund, and free front doors are worth walking through.

But **Health Innovation Networks and the NHS Innovation Accelerator are English
structures.** A Welsh project with no English partner is not their business.

**And there is a timing problem nobody mentions.** NHS England is being
abolished and folded into the Department of Health and Social Care. The decision
was announced in March 2025; the Health Bill had its second reading on 1 June
2026; the transition targets October 2026 with completion by April 2027.

So for roughly the next year, the organisation everyone recommends approaching
is dissolving itself, its staff are in a redundancy consultation, and its
functions are moving to DHSC and to integrated care boards. **This is the worst
possible moment to expect a considered reply from NHS England about a speculative
idea.** It is not a reason to stay away, but it is a reason not to read silence
as rejection, and a strong reason to prefer Wales, where no such upheaval is
happening.

[Health Bill progress, House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-11078/)

## 4. What the ChatGPT assessment got right and wrong

**Right, and worth keeping:**

- **The reframe is the good idea.** Not "will the NHS buy this" but "is there a
  problem here the NHS would help investigate". That is the correct first
  milestone and it is much more achievable.
- **The hypothesis framing is right.** You have a hypothesis and a prototype,
  not an answer, and saying so is a strength. It matches what
  [`what-is-proven.md`](what-is-proven.md) already says.
- **"What remains unsolved after SPR?" is genuinely the killer question.** It is
  the right thing to be able to answer in one sentence.
- **The Single Patient Record is real and the dates check out**: legislation
  May 2026, prototypes in 2026 starting with maternity, public access through
  the NHS App from 2028.

**Wrong or missing:**

- **It gave you an England answer.** Every named route — NHS Innovation Service,
  Health Innovation Networks, NHS Innovation Accelerator, the SME action plan —
  is NHS England. You are in Wales. This is the single biggest correction.
- **It did not mention that NHS England is being abolished.** Advice to build a
  relationship with NHS England in 2026 is advice to build a relationship with
  an organisation that will not exist in 2027.
- **It did not mention you are not a company**, which is what actually gates
  most of what it recommended.
- **On SPR scope it overstated what is known.** The SPR page does not *say* it
  excludes informal carers, advocates or About Me information — it simply does
  not mention them. "Not mentioned" is weaker evidence than "excluded", and the
  difference matters if somebody who has read the programme documents is sitting
  opposite you. **Find out properly before claiming the gap.**

## 5. What I would actually do, in order

1. **Read the Restack call text and decide honestly whether Hearth is in scope.**
   Deadline 3 November, and it is the only route open to you as an individual.
   If the answer is "nearly", say so early rather than writing hopefully.
2. **Publish a release, so "try it yourself" becomes an invitation somebody can
   accept.** This is the cheapest thing on the list and it makes every other
   conversation different. See [`what-is-proven.md`](what-is-proven.md).
3. **Ring Business Wales about UNITE.** One phone call, live money, Welsh.
4. **Email the Bevan Commission** and ask when the next cohort opens and whether
   a technical partner outside NHS Wales can be on an application.
5. **Register on the NHS Innovation Service.** Free, slow, no downside.
6. **Answer "what remains unsolved after SPR?" properly** — from the programme
   documents, not from assumption. Until that is answered, every conversation
   above is weaker than it needs to be.

**Nothing on this list needs money, and the first two need nobody's permission.**
